## Format

- **Title machine:** `What If You [superpower / impossible condition]?`,
  second person.
- **Length:** 8–10 minutes (longer than Bradikinin and Last Soul).
- **Structure** (Immortal):
  - **Hook:** an everyday moment, then a disaster (truck), then you survive.
    The rules of the power appear as animated checklist badges, then the
    title card. At 0:30 the narrator says "stick around, because by the end
    of this video, immortality is going to look very different."
  - **Limits:** testing the power, with quick visual gags.
  - **Science:** real facts (telomeres, the immortal jellyfish, Greenland
    sharks, Jeanne Calment, hydra) shown as clean animated diagrams.
  - **Fun era:** wish-fulfilment montage, including a compound-interest gag.
  - **Costs:** a friend (Max) ages while you don't, then trapped
    underground, then Max's death, then fading memories.
  - **Time jumps:** year 120, 500, 800 (the moon walk), 10,000, 5 billion
    years (the Sun becomes a red giant), trillions of years (heat death).
  - **Twist:** cyclic cosmology (Penrose) and a new universe: "round two".
  - Ends on a callback to the friend's running joke ("don't forget the
    snacks").
- **Engagement:** two mid-video questions ("drop it in the comments, I read
  them") at emotional lulls, then a closing question and "subscribe for a new
  What If story every week". Result: 450 comments on 486K views.

## Visual / audio style (per vidIQ watch)

- 2D flat vector stick figures with **solid black round heads**, simple
  white eyes and mouth, and a colour outfit per character (yellow hoodie for
  you, blue for Max).
- Warm cream and pastel daytime palette; navy and neon for science and space.
- Cut-out rigging with smooth walk cycles and head tilts; cinematic
  letterbox; zooms, pans, whip transitions, depth-of-field blur.
- Comic splash text ("CRASH!"), checklist badges, counters ("Healed in
  0.1s", "4,788 notebooks"), graphs.
- Upbeat documentary narrator, with short **character dialogue lines**
  voiced separately. A synth score that strips back to drones for sad parts.
  Lots of diegetic SFX.
- A new gag, graphic or time jump every 15–20 seconds.

## Takeaways for us

1. **Emotional core:** Bradikinin is clever; this channel makes people care
   (Max). Our files have no recurring person to lose.
2. **Real science woven into the fiction**, which makes the videos
   shareable and safe to recommend.
3. **Character voice lines** break up the narration.
4. **Comment prompts** placed at quiet moments.
5. **Longer videos** (8–10 min), held together by an escalating time jump.
